================================================================================
HOW TO READ THIS ORDER
================================================================================

Deadline: 31 July 2026, 11:59 PM ET. Roughly TWO working days.

You cannot do 45 items in two days. The PRD is a product vision, not a two-day
plan. Three rules govern the ranking:

1. CHEAP AND CATASTROPHIC GOES FIRST. #1 (SkillsBuild) is a fifteen-minute check
   that can invalidate the entire entry. #2 (watsonx) may need a credential
   escalation with hours of waiting, so it starts before anything depends on it.

2. BLOCK 4 IS NOT LOWER PRIORITY — IT IS LATER IN TIME. Those items are P0. An
   unsubmitted project scores zero regardless of the code. Reserve Day 2
   afternoon for them and do not spend it on features.

3. WHEN YOU RUN OUT OF TIME, STOP AT A RANK — do not half-do three items in
   parallel. Cut everything below where you stopped, and say so in the README
   (#39). PRD §20 names scope creep as risk #1.

Where to stop if you fall behind:
  Stop at #22 → you have the full PRD demo path, thin but real.
  Stop at #17 → you lose panel context and character refinement; still filmable.
  Stop at #12 → you have the continuity moment, which is the single most
                important 15 seconds in the video. Below that, the demo has no
                climax.

#2 · D1 · Make one real watsonx call succeed (THE SPIKE) · P0
PRD     §17, and the entire premise of entering a watsonx challenge
WHY     THE BIGGEST RISK IN THE PROJECT AND IT IS STILL OPEN. Every AI response
        in the app comes from src/lib/ai/mocks.ts. There is a concrete bug
        blocking it:

          provider.ts sends   Authorization: Bearer ${WATSONX_API_KEY}

        watsonx.ai does not accept a raw API key. You must first exchange it for
        a short-lived IAM access token:

          POST https://iam.cloud.ibm.com/identity/token
          Content-Type: application/x-www-form-urlencoded
          grant_type=urn:ibm:params:oauth:grant-type:apikey&apikey=<KEY>

        then send THAT as the bearer. As written every real call returns 401 —
        which is caught and silently replaced by a mock, which is precisely why
        nobody has noticed.

        Ranked this high partly because it may not be code-bound at all: if the
        account is not provisioned, the escalation takes hours you do not have
        later.
ACCEPT  A real generated_text returns from watsonx in a test or script, p50
        latency posted in the team chat, token cached and refreshed before expiry
        (~1h lifetime).
FILES   src/lib/ai/provider.ts
SIZE    M
BLOCKER Requires provisioned IBM Cloud credentials. IF THE ACCOUNT IS NOT
        PROVISIONED, ESCALATE IN THE FIRST HOUR — not tomorrow night.
